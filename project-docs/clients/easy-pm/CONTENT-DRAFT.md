### Facts and claims still needing confirmation

These do not prevent using the draft copy above in a review build. They must be resolved before adding the related claims or assets to the public website.

- **Coverage detail:** exact towns, counties, ZIP codes and cross-state coverage are not established by the landing page. Do not add a town list or statewide promise.
- **Other pest services:** cockroach and termite services were mentioned in an earlier prototype but are not substantiated by the current source. They are excluded.
- **Address and email:** the landing-page source does not establish the draft's chosen contact address/email. Do not silently copy the main homepage's Exton address or email into Contact without confirmation.
- **Estimates:** the landing page advertises free quotes; the main homepage notes a possible location-dependent dispatch fee. The copy says “ask about an estimate” until the precise distinction is confirmed.
- **Guarantees and warranties:** confirm current terms, exclusions, eligible services, renewal cost/conditions and approved wording before using six-month or renewable-lifetime warranty language, a best-price guarantee or any promised elimination outcome.
- **Speed and availability:** confirm actual scheduling and operating hours before adding same-day, 24/7, one-day or fixed callback claims.
- **Safety and treatment claims:** do not reuse absolute family/pet safety, harmlessness, no-damage, permanent-removal or regulatory-approval statements from the source without appropriate current verification. Preparation and re-entry guidance must come from the provider's approved instructions.
- **Credentials and people:** the landing page names Andreas Duerr and mentions qualifications/training. Confirm the current owner biography, team details, licenses and certificates before adding an owner profile or trust badges.
- **Reviews:** no rating, review count or testimonial has been approved for this draft. No review text is fabricated.
- **Blog:** this deliverable covers the index page, not new full articles. No existing post inventory has been approved from the landing-page-only source. Keep the empty state until real posts are selected and checked.
- **Forms and legal copy:** confirm the actual receiving integration, privacy policy and any necessary contact consent before activation. This draft does not invent legal terms or a privacy promise.
